Two chefs and a lot of jars

We are Melanie and Gary Hennessey. We hail from many years of kitchen working experience as chefs, and now find our passion in making and selling these unique products.

We source as many ingredients locally as possible, using many farms around our community, from fresh produce to local dry ingredients and vinegars.

We pride ourselves on having rustic products jam packed with fresh ingredients and flavor. Something for everyone at your table.
