What we grow

A wide variety of market garden vegetables, eight kinds of berries and a small orchard, all grown using organic standards.

Hardneck garlic, and a lot of it

Alongside the vegetables we grow a large crop of hardneck garlic. Look for it on our market table.

Vegetables

A wide variety of market garden vegetables through the season.

Berries

Strawberries, blueberries, raspberries, haskaps, grapes, blackberries, currants and gooseberries.

Tree fruit

A small orchard of apple, pear and plum trees.

Sheep and hens

A herd of Shetland sheep, kept for wool, and a flock of free-range heritage hens decorate our lawns and fields.

Paul and Marilyn

Paul is a pharmacist and Marilyn is a veterinarian, and we love farming on our 50 acre property in the heart of the agricultural district of the Annapolis Valley.

The farm sits at the base of the North Mountain in the community of Grafton, just north of Waterville.

During the growing season we invite farm helpers, called WWOOFers, to stay with us for a few weeks at a time and help with the garden chores.

2017

Our farm was the first in Kings County to have a conservation easement placed on it by the Annapolis Valley Farm Land Trust Society.
