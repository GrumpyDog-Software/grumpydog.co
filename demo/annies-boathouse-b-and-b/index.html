Digby's life saving station

Construction of Digby's Life Saving Station began in 1911. The original crew was eight men and the lifeboat Daring.

The building held a cradle and marine railway for the lifeboat, the supplies and tools for the job, and living quarters for the crew. The station operated until it closed in 1970.

Come see the history and enjoy the view.

The beach is right here

It is a peaceful spot, with a breeze coming in off the water to keep cool. On good days you can watch whales, dolphins and grey seals swim by from the deck.
